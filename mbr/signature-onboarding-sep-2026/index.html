Reporting Change: Signature Onboarding Now Attributed to Industry Cloud

Success Plans span Sales, Service, Industries & Revenue Cloud on a single platform — prior completions were logged under Sales Cloud by default. From June 2026, onboarding is attributed to Industry & Revenue Cloud when it carries the highest AOV, giving sub-clouds a true picture of their onboarding footprint. This closes a long-standing reporting gap and enables accurate cloud-level benchmarking going forward.
