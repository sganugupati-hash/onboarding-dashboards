Case Comments & Notes Analysis
Login Issues — 30%
Customers encounter login problems on Help & Training or their Salesforce instance. A common first blocker that delays onboarding progress.
Scheduling Friction
Appointment Scheduler issues and customers going unresponsive after initial engagement are the primary driver of cancellations (14.2% cancel rate in Q3).
Strong Follow-On Signals — 27%
Roughly 1 in 4 Q3 customers show Guide Handover signals in case comments. A few also request post-90-day check-ins — a warm pipeline for Guide engagement.
Language Requirement — 10%
~10% of customers request Spanish-language delivery. Mexico Success Engineers are now delivering Onboarding in Q3 (5 cases in progress).
Out-of-Scope / Asks: A subset of customers — particularly Nonprofit Cloud — arrive expecting set-up and configuration assistance rather than guided onboarding. Clear expectation-setting at intake is an Onboarding 2.0 focus area.
Opportunity: 4 in 10 onboarded accounts have an Industry Guide case, but only 3 in 10 have one created after onboarding closes. Strengthening the Onboarding → Guide handoff is the clearest lever to grow post-onboarding engagement.
